# Portfolio Website

This context describes the language for Dimas Arya Murdiyan's portfolio website. The site exists to help hiring visitors understand his mobile engineering fit and take a next action.

## Language

**Hiring Visitor**:
A recruiter, hiring manager, engineering manager, or peer engineer evaluating whether Dimas is relevant for a mobile engineering opportunity.
_Avoid_: User, viewer, audience

**Positioning**:
The short professional claim the site makes about Dimas's specialty, seniority, and target opportunities.
_Avoid_: Bio, tagline, headline

**Proof Point**:
A specific evidence item that supports the positioning, such as a metric, shipped product, platform constraint, or engineering responsibility.
_Avoid_: Achievement, highlight, bullet point

**Project**:
A shipped or demonstrable product case that shows Dimas's engineering scope, technical choices, and impact.
_Avoid_: Work sample, app, portfolio item

**Experience**:
A professional role at a company or client, including scope, dates, responsibilities, and outcomes.
_Avoid_: Job, employment history

**Conversion Action**:
A deliberate action the site wants a hiring visitor to take, such as downloading the resume, opening LinkedIn, viewing GitHub, or sending email.
_Avoid_: CTA, button click, engagement
